## 🧭 About

Quant-minded software engineer in the making — a first-year Computer Science & Engineering student at **Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology (TIET), Patiala**, building toward a career at the intersection of **capital markets and applied engineering**.

Most of my work lives in Python: research platforms that turn raw market data into DCF valuations, factor-based risk models, and backtested trading strategies — engineered with the same rigor as production software, not just notebooks. That means modular architecture, dependency management across Python 3.12+, and automated test suites (up to 72 tests on a single project) validating every model against independently-derived reference calculations.

Alongside quant work, I build **applied ML and computer vision systems** — from a U-Net satellite change-detection model to a Roboflow-trained object detector deployed live on a Raspberry Pi — and **product-style tools** using React and Streamlit when an idea needs a real interface, not just a script.

**🎯 Open To**
- Summer Analyst opportunities in Global Banking & Global Markets
- Quantitative research collaborations and open-source contributions
- Conversations about portfolio construction, risk modelling, and applied ML
